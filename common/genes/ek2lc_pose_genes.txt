# EK2 Loincloths: pose genes. Hand-written (not made by gen_script_files.py).
# One morph gene per kneeling animation, built like vanilla's gene_seated (10_genes_special_misc.txt): the animation
# entry switches it on through a portrait modifier (gfx/portraits/portrait_modifiers/ek2lc_pose.txt), and our pieces
# carry a matching blend shape (<piece>_bs_kneel1.mesh / _bs_kneel2.mesh) that moves them out of the kneeling body.
# The _fat genes carry the extra move a heavy body needs (<piece>_bs_kneel1_fat.mesh), set by weight like vanilla's
# bs_body_fat_1 (99_special.txt `weight`: scope:weight_for_portrait for overweight characters).
# The attributes are EK2's unused whiskers_* names: CK3 resolves a gene's attribute only on the body or head entity
# (vanilla declares bs_body_seated on the body for this), EK2's heads declare these with a neutral shape and nothing
# else uses them. Our pieces map them to their kneel shapes; see source/maya/kneel.py.
