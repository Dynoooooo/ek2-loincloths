# Festival wrap redesigned; Meshy MCP server added

**Done** (built and looked at in Blender.app; not yet seen in game)
- The Loincloth Festival's white wrap looked like underwear. New design: red-and-white twisted rope belt with a knot at
  the back, a long white front apron with a red sunburst and red hem bands and a fringe, a wide back flap that hides the
  strip over the buttocks, and three zigzag paper streamers on each hip. New mesh and texture (no reuse).
- Meshy MCP server: wrapper ~/.local/bin/meshy-mcp (loads the secret from ~/.zshrc, expects MESHY_API_KEY), entry
  "meshy" in claude_desktop_config.json. Needs a Claude Desktop restart.
