# Step 6: kids' set (seen in game)

**Done** (commit: the one that adds this file; Claude Code)
- CK3's boy and girl bodies are the adult male and female meshes with the `bs_body_infant_1` shape and shorter bones (`boy_body.asset`, `girl_body.asset`). So every piece (all looks, all cultures) got an infant blend shape in `build_reach_hide.py` and fits children; no separate child meshes.
- Clipping: the groin-fold push-out that fixed the Lilmothiit shape now also runs for the infant and dwarf shapes. Every piece is clear of the body on every shape, which also fixes the old dwarf poke-through (2 points).
- Genes: `boy`/`girl` now get the same pieces as `male`/`female` (they were `empty`). Girls get the chest band like women.
- Who wears what (`gen_script_files.py`):
  - commoners from age 4 (under 4: vanilla's small-children clothes), girls always with the chest band;
  - the bare-chested option (Argonian, Kothringi, Lilmothiit women, "Show nudity" on) needs `is_adult = yes`, so it never applies to girls;
  - war and armoured looks from 14 (`ek2lc_war_role_trigger` now has `age >= 14` instead of `is_adult = yes`);
  - royal looks for child rulers from 4 (`ek2lc_royal_trigger` no longer needs adulthood; spouses as before).
- For children the `no_clothes` template gives vanilla's bedchamber nightshirt, not the fig leaf. `zz_ek2lc_figleaf.txt` now also redefines the two nightshirt accessories (same tag trick: nothing while our loincloth is worn, vanilla's own entity otherwise, vanilla's `set_tags` kept).
- The nightshirt's shrink tags made bare torsos and arms collapse (EK2's `special_shrink_body` turns them into body shapes). Every wearing modifier now sets `gene_shrink_body` to 0 (`mode = add`; `replace` is refused for special genes, as error.log says).
- In game: a Reach boy (8) bare-chested in the hide loincloth, normal arms (`progress/screens/kid_reach_boy.jpg`); an Argonian girl (6) in the Argonian chest band and loincloth (`progress/screens/kid_argonian_girl.jpg`). error.log: nothing new about our files.
- Dropped the stashed Copilot edit (adults only on war/royal); superseded by the above.

**Problems and workarounds**

- Barbershop "Clothes" shows the accessory's name: a child in our pieces shows "Nightgown" (hidden), an adult "No Clothes".
