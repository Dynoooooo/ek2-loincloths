**Problems and workarounds**
- `make_textures()` makes NaN in the cord row: float32 `sin(π)` is slightly below 0, and that to the power 0.6 is NaN. It's pre-existing and harmless after DDS export. `undead.py` applies `nan_to_num` before working on a texture.
- Shell commands that redirect into files (heredocs, `>>`), and direct `/opt/homebrew/bin/magick`, were denied here. Python's own subprocess calls and the file tools worked.
