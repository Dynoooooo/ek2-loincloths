# Kneel shapes for every piece (plain and heavy body)

**Done** (branch `fix/kneel-shape`, not committed yet)
- Kneels now switch on pose shapes, as thrones do. `gfx/portraits/portrait_animations/zz_ek2lc_kneel.txt` redefines
  `throne_room_kneel_1`/`_2` (EK2 0.21.2's entries + `ek2lc_pose = kneel1/kneel2`; user OK'd). Shown in game with a
  test marker: the redefined entries and their modifier are used.
- `portrait_modifiers/ek2lc_pose.txt` sets `gene_ek2lc_kneel1/2` to 1, and `gene_ek2lc_kneel1/2_fat` to
  `scope:weight_for_portrait` for overweight characters (vanilla's `weight` modifier drives `bs_body_fat_1` that way).
- Genes need their attribute on the body or head entity ("could not find attribute" otherwise). Those files may not
  be replaced, so the genes drive EK2's unused neutral head attributes `whiskers_thin_1/thin_2/droop/wavy`
  (`kneel.free_attrs()` lists the free ones). error.log is clean.
- `source/maya/kneel.py` (`mayapy source/tools/kneel_run.py`): body as one solid (winding number + distance grid),
  cloth carried from rest into the pose in 16 steps, edges kept between posed and rest length, poke-through pass,
  smoothing over the welded mesh. 273 + 236 plain shapes, 274 + 251 heavy ones; logs in `source/build_logs/`.
- Vertices inside the kneeling body, all pieces: kneel1 307,847 -> 5,250, kneel2 105,320 -> 3,071 (heavy body:
  271,238 -> 26,278 and 152,838 -> 37,497). Maya audit of 12 pieces (`audit.measure`): 0 inside on the plain body in
  both kneels; heavy body 0 except khajiit hide kneel2 9 (1.0 cm), m_reach_king 5, m_reach_armour 2-5.
- Throne and cross-legged poses are unchanged (shapes are off there; skin weights untouched).
- Seen in game by the user (barbershop, Scene, Kneeling): fixed. So CK3 applies a head-declared attribute to
  body attachments too.
