# Research and designs: lich pieces, new loincloth peoples, their celebrations (2026-11-09)

Exa research (UESP lore and museum/academic sources). Every piece below is its own new mesh and texture (the user:
"I want completely original assets").

## Sources, short
- Tibetan *rus rgyan* bone aprons: net of carved bone beads, plaques at the waistband, worn in charnel-ground rites as a
  reminder of death (Huntington Archive; Rubin Museum; MEG Geneva; MOA).
- Moche lords of Sipán: gold and copper *backflaps* (tumi-shaped plates) hung from the belt, rattles and bells at the
  belt (Smarthistory; latinamericanstudies; NYT 1994).
- Egyptian funerary bead nets: lozenge net of blue tube beads, ring beads at the joins, winged scarab and amulets
  (MFA Boston; Met; Art Institute of Chicago).
- TES liches: Mannimarco's Necromancer's Moon (Revenant) eclipses Arkay every eight days, black soul gems; Ayleid liches
  (Celemaril); meteoric glass, welkynd and varla stones; Dragon Priest masks and layered hide knots; draugr wrappings.
- TES giants: loincloths or pelts with bones, braided beaded hair, spiral scars, troll skulls on belts, marked mammoth
  tusks; regular gathering at a ceremonial site to trade and mate; sacred burial grounds; mammoth funerals.
- TES Keptu: warrior-smiths earn ink markings; annual hunt competition; bears given Hircine's marks each season;
  Bloodroot Forge (lava, nirncrux).
- TES Nedes (Perena, Duraki): Cult of the Stars, the Celestials (Warrior, Mage, Thief), nirncrux.
- TES Yokudans: Way of the Sword, sword-singers taken at eleven, belts that carry tassets, broad-arrowhead motif;
  Drigh R'Zimb, the jubilation for the sun Daibethe in the hottest season.
- Ceremony structure: Ainu *iyomante* (raising, sending off, decorated skull, feast); Matariki (viewing the stars and
  reading the year, calling the names of the dead, feeding the stars); Inti Raymi (three days' fast, new fire lit with
  a concave gold mirror, offerings, feast); royal Egyptian shendyt (pleated kilt, central apron, tail at the back).
